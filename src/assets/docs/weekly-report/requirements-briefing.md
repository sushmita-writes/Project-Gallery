# Project: Gallery

Gallery is an e-commerse website where customers can browse and purchase high-quality Print-On-Demand Art prints from diverse artists and themes/genres.

## Week 1: Requirements Gathering

### Client-side Story

At Gallery, customers are able to:

+ **Browse** art prints that are categorised by themes/genres (e.g., abstract, contemporary, nature, fantasy)
+ **Search by**: Categories, title, or artist
+ **Filter by**: Price range
+ **View product** details, including: Images, description, artist name, medium, and dimensions, Customer reviews/ratings
+ **Create an account**
+ **Log in** to their account 
+ View and Manage their **User dashboard** that contains:
    - Order history
    - Order status
    - Saved cart items
    - Account settings
+ **Add to cart** or buy directly
+ Proceed payments with E-wallets (NPR currency)
+ Receive **email notifications** for:
    - Order confirmation
    - Order status

#### NOTE: 
- *No registration required for browsing/searching/viewing products*  
- *Registration required to add items to cart, and to proceed to checkout*

### Users

1. Admin
+ Add/edit/delete prints and descriptions
+ Upload images
+ Process orders
+ Update status
+ Customer Support

2. Users (Customers)

### Product 

+ **Licensed Artworks:** Artists retain copyrights, and customers are prohibited from redistributing or reselling the licensed artworks
+ **Public Domain Artworks:** Copyright-free artworks from the public domain, available for unrestricted use
